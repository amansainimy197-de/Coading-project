FAQ

Questions, answered

How fast can you deliver?

Most landing sites go live in 5–7 days. Larger multi-page projects take 2–3 weeks depending on content readiness.

Will the site work on mobile?

Yes. Every page is designed mobile-first and tested on real phones and tablets.

Can I edit the content myself?

Yes. Colours, text and images are simple to change, and we can add a CMS if you want one.

Do you provide hosting?

We can deploy to Vercel, Netlify or your own host, and guide you through domain setup.

What about revisions?

Each plan includes revision rounds. Extra changes are quoted fairly and upfront.
