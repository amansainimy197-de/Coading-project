Learning path

Your 3D roadmap

STEP 01

Learn the basics in the browser

Start with Spline or Vectary to understand scenes, lights, materials and interaction without installing anything.

STEP 02

Go deep with Blender

Learn modeling, UV unwrapping, sculpting and rendering with the free, open-source Blender.

STEP 03

Add realistic materials

Use Adobe Substance 3D to author PBR textures and export them for real-time engines.

STEP 04

Source and share assets

Browse Sketchfab, Poly Pizza, CGTrader and Thangs for models; publish your own work to get feedback.

STEP 05

Ship it on the web

Export optimized GLB/glTF, embed in HTML with Three.js or model-viewer, and test performance on mobile.
